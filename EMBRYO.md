# Embryo RTOS

Embryo RTOS is Embryo-Zero's fork of [zephyr](https://github.com/zephyrproject-rtos/zephyr) (29d3d75b7, commit 29d3d75b7). It does operating system for small boards.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
